# BRAND_CONTEXT

## Mission
HoopWithHer exists to expand access, visibility, and development pathways for girls basketball athletes through trusted digital tools, programming, and community infrastructure. The platform seeks to close the resource and exposure gap in female sports by providing a consolidated, premium experience for athletes, families, and coaches.

## Elite GBB role
Elite GBB serves as the evaluation, readiness, and high-performance layer for player development, exposure, and progression planning. It acts as the premium tier, unlocking advanced analytics, direct scouting evaluations, and personalized recruiting roadmaps.

## ConnectGBB role
ConnectGBB serves as the network/community layer for athlete discovery, connections, and communication workflows. It provides the social and connective tissue allowing players, coaches, and recruiters to interact safely within a managed ecosystem.

## HoopWithHer TV / Media
The TV and media arm serves to amplify athlete stories, broadcast event highlights, and provide educational content. It is the distribution engine for brand awareness and athlete visibility.

## Brand voice
- **Clear, empowering, and practical:** Use active verbs and direct language. Avoid jargon that alienates parents or younger athletes.
- **Athlete-first and family-respectful:** Tone should always center the athlete's journey while providing transparency to the family.
- **Data-informed without being exclusionary:** Present metrics and evaluations constructively as tools for growth, not definitive ceilings.
- **Positive, credible, and future-focused:** Maintain a professional, encouraging aesthetic that inspires confidence in the program's ability to drive next-level success.

## Target audiences
- Athletes (middle school, high school, post-grad)
- Parents/guardians
- Coaches and program directors
- Evaluators/scouts
- Partners/sponsors

## Privacy note for minors
Any product surface involving minors must apply strict privacy defaults. Public pages must avoid exposing sensitive personal data, direct contacts, or private evaluation notes. PII (Personally Identifiable Information) such as phone numbers, personal emails, or home addresses must remain strictly gated behind authenticated role-based access controls (RBAC) and must never appear in public profiles or leaderboards.

## Visual direction
Modern, clean, high-contrast sports aesthetic with strong hierarchy, accessible typography, and consistent HoopWithHer brand color usage. Interfaces should feel premium, dynamic, and action-oriented.
