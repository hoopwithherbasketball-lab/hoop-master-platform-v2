# BRAND_CONTEXT

> Status: Foundation placeholder. This file will be expanded in Phase 1: Bootstrap Target Docs.

## Mission
HoopWithHer exists to expand access, visibility, and development pathways for girls basketball athletes through trusted digital tools, programming, and community infrastructure.

## Elite GBB role
Elite GBB serves as the evaluation, readiness, and high-performance layer for player development, exposure, and progression planning.

## ConnectGBB role
ConnectGBB serves as the network/community layer for athlete discovery, connections, and communication workflows.

## Brand voice
- Clear, empowering, and practical
- Athlete-first and family-respectful
- Data-informed without being exclusionary
- Positive, credible, and future-focused

## Target audiences
- Athletes (middle school, high school, post-grad)
- Parents/guardians
- Coaches and program directors
- Evaluators/scouts
- Partners/sponsors

## Privacy note for minors
Any product surface involving minors must apply strict privacy defaults. Public pages must avoid exposing sensitive personal data, direct contacts, or private evaluation notes.

## Visual direction
Modern, clean, high-contrast sports aesthetic with strong hierarchy, accessible typography, and consistent HoopWithHer brand color usage.
