## 1. Project Overview

**HWH Player Advantage™** is a recruiting platform for Hoop With Her, supporting basketball athletes (middle school and high school, girls and boys). The system handles:

- **Player Data Intake** — Multi-step public form to collect player info, stats, evaluations, media links, and package selection.
- **Admin Dashboard** — Kanban pipeline board, player directory, project management, deliverable generation, coach management, event management,)
