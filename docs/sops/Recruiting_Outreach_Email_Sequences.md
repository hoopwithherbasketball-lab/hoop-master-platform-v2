# HOOP WITH HER Recruiting Outreach Email Sequences

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the coach-outreach email sequences, message templates, scenario variations, and CRM triggers used by HOOP WITH HER recruiting services. The goal is to make outreach repeatable without turning it into generic mass email.

These templates are written for girls basketball athletes and families moving through the recruiting process from intake to school-fit targeting, coach outreach, event communication, follow-up, and decision support.

## 2. Operating Rules

- Athlete communication should stay in the athlete's voice whenever possible.
- Parents and staff may review, coach, and quality-control outreach, but should not misrepresent coach interest.
- Do not guarantee scholarship money, roster spots, offers, admissions, visits, or coach responses.
- Every coach email should include a verified profile link, usable film, grad year, position, location, academics when strong or requested, and the reason the school is on the target list.
- Avoid automated mass blasting. Use segmented, personalized outreach tied to realistic school fit.
- Keep parent phone numbers, private evaluation notes, and sensitive academic details out of public or broadly forwarded messages.
- Track every send, reply, follow-up date, and next action in the recruiting CRM.

## 4. Recommended CRM Stages

| Stage | Trigger | Required Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `profile_building` | Athlete enrolled but asset stack is incomplete | Complete profile, film, academics, schedule, and evaluation |
| `target_list_review` | Evaluation and first school list are ready | Athlete/family approves reach, target, and foundation schools |
| `ready_for_initial_outreach` | Profile, film, and target list are approved | Send intro emails to prioritized programs |
| `initial_outreach_sent` | First email sent | Schedule follow-up for 7-10 days |
| `follow_up_due` | No response after first send | Send value-added follow-up, not "checking in" only |
| `coach_engaged` | Coach replies, watches film, asks a question, sends questionnaire, or invites to camp | Log engagement and assign next action |
| `event_notice_due` | Athlete has upcoming exposure event | Send pre-event schedule to target programs |
| `post_event_follow_up_due` | Event completed | Send results, updated film, and next schedule |
| `monthly_update_due` | Active recruiting cycle continues | Send concise progress update |
| `closed_or_paused` | Athlete commits, season ends, family pauses, or program is no longer fit | Document reason and next review date |

## 5. Core Sequences

### Sequence A: Recruiting Launch

Use after enrollment, before coach outreach.

| Step | Timing | Audience | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Day 0 | Parent and athlete | Confirm enrollment, expectations, and required assets |
| A2 | Day 2 | Athlete | Request missing profile, film, schedule, and academic details |
| A3 | Day 7 | Parent and athlete | Confirm asset stack status and next deadline |
| A4 | Day 10-14 | Parent and athlete | Share readiness summary and target-list work plan |

#### A1. Enrollment Confirmation

**Subject options**

- Your HOOP WITH HER recruiting launch plan
- Next steps for {{athlete_first_name}}'s recruiting process
- Welcome to the HOOP WITH HER recruiting program

**Template**

Hi {{parent_first_name}} and {{athlete_first_name}},

Welcome to the HOOP WITH HER recruiting process. Over the next few weeks, we are going to organize the information college coaches need, build or refine {{athlete_first_name}}'s recruiting profile, review film, and create a realistic school-fit plan.

Our first priority is the recruiting asset stack:

- verified athlete profile
- academic snapshot
- highlight film
- full-game film
- current schedule
- evaluation notes
- initial target-school list

This process does not guarantee coach responses, offers, roster spots, scholarship money, or admission. What we will provide is a disciplined, trackable recruiting workflow with clearer positioning and consistent accountability.

Please complete the intake items by {{deadline_date}} so we can move into profile review and target-list planning.

Best,
{{staff_name}}

#### A2. Missing Assets Reminder

**Subject options**

- Missing items for {{athlete_first_name}}'s recruiting profile
- Action needed: recruiting profile materials
- Quick checklist before coach outreach

**Template**

Hi {{athlete_first_name}},

Before we contact college programs, we need your recruiting information to be coach-ready. Right now we still need:

{{missing_items_list}}

Once these are complete, we can review your profile, film, and school-fit list with more confidence. Outreach works best when a coach can quickly understand who you are, where you play, what level you are targeting, and how to evaluate your film.

Please upload or send these items by {{deadline_date}}.

Best,
{{staff_name}}

### Sequence B: Target List Approval

Use after evaluation and before the first school outreach.

| Step | Timing | Audience | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | When first list is ready | Parent and athlete | Present reach, target, and foundation schools |
| B2 | 48 hours later | Parent and athlete | Collect approval and preferences |
| B3 | After approval | Athlete | Prepare athlete's outreach voice |

#### B1. School-Fit List Review

**Subject options**

- {{athlete_first_name}}'s first recruiting target list
- School-fit review for {{athlete_first_name}}
- Reach, target, and foundation programs for review

**Template**

Hi {{parent_first_name}} and {{athlete_first_name}},

We have prepared the first recruiting target list for {{athlete_first_name}}. The list is organized by reach, target, and foundation programs so we can be ambitious while still staying realistic.

Each school was reviewed for a mix of athletic fit, academic fit, location, likely roster opportunity, affordability considerations, and available program information.

Please review the attached list and mark:

- programs you are excited about
- programs you do not want to pursue
- geography or academic preferences we should adjust
- any family budget considerations we need to account for

Once we confirm the list, we will begin the first wave of coach outreach.

Best,
{{staff_name}}

#### B3. Athlete Voice Prep

**Subject options**

- Preparing your first coach emails
- Your outreach voice matters
- Before we contact college coaches

**Template**

Hi {{athlete_first_name}},

Your first coach email should sound professional, confident, and like you. Coaches do not need a long life story in the first message. They need the essentials and a clear reason you are interested in their program.

For each school, we will personalize around:

- why the program fits you
- what you bring as a player
- your profile and film
- your upcoming schedule
- a clear next step

Please send me 2-3 sentences in your own words about what you want college coaches to know about your game, work ethic, and college goals.

Best,
{{staff_name}}

## 7. Variations by Recruiting Stage

| Stage | Tone | Best Message Type | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freshman/Foundation | Developmental, introduction-focused | Short intro, development updates, camp interest | Asking for offer-level feedback too early |
| Sophomore/Positioning | Progress-focused | Film update, event schedule, academic profile | Overstating level before evaluation |
| Junior/Active | Direct and specific | Targeted fit email, event schedule, full-game film | Generic "please recruit me" emails |
| Senior/Urgent | Clear and time-aware | Availability, roster fit, complete film package | Waiting too long between follow-ups |
| High-academic | Academic and athletic fit | GPA, major interest, rigorous school fit | Sending GPA if not approved or not useful |
| Needs development | Honest growth narrative | Improvement plan and updated film | Hiding gaps coaches will notice |

## 8. Staff Quality-Control Checklist

Before any coach email goes out, confirm:

- The athlete profile link works.
- Film links are accessible without a private login unless intentionally restricted.
- The coach's name, school, and program are correct.
- The email names one specific fit reason.
- The athlete's graduation year, position, height, location, and school are accurate.
- Any academic claim is approved and current.
- Parent contact details and private notes are not included unless explicitly appropriate.
- The CRM has a next follow-up date.
- The message does not imply guaranteed placement or staff influence with the college program.

## 10. Platform Implementation Status

Implemented in the current platform:

- This SOP remains the operating reference for recruiting staff.
- Typed stages, templates, variations, variables, and rendering utilities live under `packages/features/src/recruiting`.
- The admin-only Recruiting Outreach Center lives at `/admin/recruiting/outreach`.
- Staff can filter by stage and scenario, personalize a draft, detect missing fields, and copy the result for review.
- The current release does not send email or create delivery records.

Future controlled additions:

1. Add persistent, editable template overrides with version history.
2. Add athlete-specific draft records, approval status, and follow-up tasks.
3. Add send history and engagement tracking with strict ownership and role policies.
4. Add email delivery only after explicit approval, verified sender identity, consent, unsubscribe/compliance rules, and parent visibility are implemented.

Do not send real emails automatically until permissions, identity, unsubscribe/compliance needs, and parent visibility rules are approved.
